## Poetry

Writing is my other way of exploring the unknown, driven by the same relentless curiosity that led me to pursuing Physics at University. My poetry dwells in the spaces between what we know, what we cannot yet name and what we refuse to acknowledge.

I write in free verse, finding that form without rigid structure or rhythm best captures the chaotic motion of thoughts and feelings.
