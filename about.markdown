## Who I Am

My name is Alicia Pickard and I am a Physics Master's Student at the University of Exeter. I am fascinated by the origins of life and the challenge of discovering it beyond Earth, with a focus on using computational modelling and scientific programming to aid in this search. Alongside my academic pursuits, I write as another way of exploring the unknown and navigating life's complexities. This site brings together the two sides of my work: analytical and technical while also creative and expressive.
