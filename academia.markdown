## Master's Research

My Master's project seeks to establish whether the physical and chemical properties inferred from JWST disk spectra using DuCKLinG are consistent with those obtained from full thermo-chemical simulations. My particular focus is on determining how accurately DuCKLinG can recover information about complex hydrocarbons and molecules relevant to prebiotic chemistry.

### Research Objectives

**I. Benchmarking DuCKLinG against thermo-chemical models**

Using synthetic spectra generated from detailed models as a benchmark dataset to compare retrieved parameters against known values, assessing accuracy and bias.

**II. Diagnostic fidelity for complex hydrocarbons**

Testing how well DuCKLinG can recover molecules such as acetylene (C₂H₂), hydrogen cyanide (HCN), and more complex hydrocarbons relevant to the chemistry of life's building blocks.

**III. Quantifying model limitations**

Identifying where the simplified model performs well and where its assumptions break down, defining clear applicability regimes.
